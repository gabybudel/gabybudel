I am an Applied Scientist at Uber with a Ph.D. in Artificial Intelligence and Network Science from TU Delft. My doctoral research focused on embeddings of complex networks — the roles of complementarity and similarity — with applications to semantic networks and NLP. My interests include machine learning, causal inference, hyperbolic graph embeddings, deep learning, and NLP.
